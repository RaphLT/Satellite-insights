Selling satellite insight: how a data signal loses value as more funds buy it

The decision this prototype supports: how many funds should a vendor like Orbital Insight sell its parking-lot signal to, at what price, and can that business ever carry the ~$130M of venture capital the company raised? Then: would a new entrant do better selling to customers who don't need exclusivity?

4. Selling exclusivity vs selling an answer

Alpha-signal revenue peaks and falls as clients erode each other's edge; revenue from non-rival customers grows with every client.

The four curves

Slow 1/(1+0.1(k−1)): the edge fades gently. Fixed pie 1/k: a fixed amount of mispricing split equally. Competition 4/(k+1)²: funds race to trade first and together earn less than one exclusive fund would (Cournot competition with linear price impact). Fast e−0.3(k−1): the edge collapses after a few clients. Price per client = that fund's yearly trading profit minus its own processing cost, assuming the vendor captures the whole surplus, so all revenues are upper bounds.
